# Run Walk Cycle

**Run, walk, repeat. A free interval timer that takes you from your first run to 30 minutes non-stop in 8 weeks.**

**Open the app:** https://ianyarko.github.io/run-walk-cycle/

---

## Why this exists

Most beginners quit running because they start too hard. Run/walk intervals fix that: run for a short burst, walk to recover, repeat. Run Walk Cycle times the intervals for you and calls them out loud, so you can keep your eyes on the road instead of your phone.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| **8-week plan** | 24 sessions, three a week, building from 1-minute runs to 30 minutes non-stop |
| **Your own intervals** | Set any run, walk and round count, with optional warm-up and cool-down |
| **Voice and beeps** | "Run", "Walk", halfway and every kilometre, read out loud. Works with your music playing |
| **GPS distance** | Distance, pace and kilometre splits while you run |
| **How did it feel?** | Mark a run Easy, Good or Hard. A hard session repeats before you move on |
| **History** | Every run with distance, time and splits, plus personal bests and your week streak |
| **Share card** | A picture of your run, ready for your WhatsApp status |
| **Pocket mode** | A black screen that ignores taps in your pocket while the timer, voice and GPS keep going. Hold the circle for 2 seconds to unlock |
| **Ghana vibes** | Funny voice lines and photos (“Run! Pretend the trotro is leaving you.”, “Ayekoo!”). Switch it off in Settings for plain callouts |
| **Backup & restore** | Save all your runs to one file and bring them back on a new phone |

## Getting started

1. Open **https://ianyarko.github.io/run-walk-cycle/**
   - iPhone: in **Safari**, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**
   - Android: in **Chrome**, tap **⋮ → Install app**
2. Open it from the home-screen icon and allow **location** when asked, so it can measure distance.
3. Put your earphones in, tap **Start**, and follow the voice.

**Tips**
- Tap **Pocket mode** before the phone goes in your pocket. It keeps the timer going and stops accidental taps.
- Save a backup every week or two in **Settings → Backup**.
- Keep the screen on during a run for the most accurate distance. The app tries to stop the screen from sleeping.
- On iPhone, turn off silent mode to hear the beeps.

## Privacy

- No account and no server. Your runs are saved on your own phone.
- Your location is only used during a run to measure distance, and never leaves your phone.
- Backups are saved as a file on your phone. Keep it somewhere safe; it’s yours.
- The app counts anonymous visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) (no cookies, no personal data).

## Releasing an update

1. Upload the new `index.html`.
2. In `sw.js`, bump the version line (e.g. `run-walk-cycle-v2` → `run-walk-cycle-v3`) and upload it too.
